# Mixmax

> Mixmax is the AI sales execution platform that helps revenue teams close more deals with less busywork.

## Products

- **Inbox Copilot** ($29/user/mo): AI inbox management — prioritizes messages, surfaces buyer signals, drafts follow-ups
- **Meeting Copilot** ($29/user/mo): Meeting intelligence — pre-call prep, real-time capture, instant summaries, CRM sync
- **Engagement Copilot** ($49/user/mo): Multi-channel sequences — personalized email, phone, and LinkedIn outreach
- **Mixmax Suite** ($89/user/mo): All three copilots in one package
- **Mixmax Free** ($0): Email tracking (20/mo), basic scheduling, 1,500 sequence recipients

## Key Capabilities

- Email tracking (opens, clicks, replies)
- One-click meeting scheduling with round-robin
- Multi-channel sales sequences (email, phone, LinkedIn)
- Workflow automation and Salesforce sync
- Real-time buyer intent signals
- AI-powered email drafting and meeting summaries
- 40+ integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoom, Slack, LinkedIn, and more)

## Differentiators

- Works natively inside Gmail and Outlook — no separate app to learn
- AI surfaces "next best action" rather than just adding more tasks
- Three specialized copilots for inbox, meetings, and engagement
- Inbox-centric design vs. dashboard-centric competitors

## Key Metrics

- 10,000+ revenue teams
- 1,000+ 5-star reviews on G2 and Capterra
- Customers report: 85% email open rates, 40% reply rates, 90 hours/week saved
- SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR compliant
